THE PLATFORM

A common picture.
A clear boundary.

Source entities retain their operational data. AIRS normalizes permitted observations, preserves their origin and freshness, and represents them for authorized incident participants.

01

Connect entities

Discover entities in the directory. Activate standing Partner permissions, invite incident-only Associates, and include Participants through dispatch, radio, phone, or liaison.

02

Understand the incident

See authorized locations, assignments, hazards, objectives, and meaningful changes together. Unknown, stale, unavailable, and conflicting information remain visible.

03

Close the sharing loop

End incident sharing when the response closes. Keep collaboration tied to the mission, rather than permanent access.

START INSIDE AIRS AGENT

Your entity.
Your systems. Your choices.

Guided onboarding captures your entity profile, contacts, service area, and capabilities. Platform-specific follow-up questions build a reusable Source Systems profile.

01

Authorize ingestion

For each source, decide whether AIRS may ingest authorized operational information. Identifying a system does not connect it or share its data.

02

Choose a practical path

The acquisition model supports native APIs and streams, secure tunnels, authorized web adapters, structured transport, and human reporting. Availability depends on the source, permissions, and verified integration.

03

Keep source ownership

Participants see the AIRS representation. Specialized source-platform access is supplemental, entity/event scoped, and tracked through provisioning and revocation.

COLLABORATION BETWEEN ENTITIES

Three ways
to take part.

Partner

A standing relationship with a defined sharing envelope. Incident type, source, information class, and approval rules determine what can activate.

Associate

An entity invited for an incident chooses temporary sharing. No permanent Partner relationship is required.

Participant

An entity contributes operationally through dispatch, radio, phone, liaison, email, or manual reporting, even without a connected source system or AIRS account.

FROM ACTIVATION TO CLOSEOUT

Useful context.
Explicit limits.

AIRS starts operationally empty. Public reference information and integration knowledge provide a foundation; authorized incident activity supplies the operational picture.

01

Preserve the evidence behind the picture

Source identity, timestamps, verification, precision, and sharing scope travel with observations. Correlation retains the underlying reports.

02

Show what is missing

Information gaps and conflicting reports deserve attention. A reported victim without a represented location remains an explicit operational gap.

03

End incident sharing

Closeout ends incident grants, expires temporary authorizations, and tracks external revocation. Cached data expires under policy; designated evidence and necessary audit history are retained.
